About

Sports Media Producer & Editor

London based from October 2026 · Right to work in the UK

I'm a University of Edinburgh graduate with First Class Honours in Linguistics, which gave me a global outlook and a real appreciation for working across cultures. My work spans documentary, client video, short-form content, and live sports production.

This summer I interned with the USTA's Video Services team and stayed on as a PA through the US Open, where I pitched, produced, and edited my own work for the US Open, USTA, and WTA social channels.

As President of Edinburgh University Television, I led the station to a record 8 nominations and 6 wins at national awards, including recognition from the Royal Television Society and NaSTA. I also write, with bylines in Hey Alma and The Boston Globe.

Outside of work, I'm a big Boston sports fan, love a good pub quiz, and am always happy to share fun language facts with anyone who will listen.
